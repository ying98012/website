A lightweight inventory system for small businesses and solo founders: local-first, order-driven stock changes, multi-unit conversion with weighted-average cost, and dual-track on-device / cloud AI for business insights.

- Create purchase records immediately; stock moves only after inbound/outbound confirmation; ledgers are traceable and can be voided in reverse
- Multi-unit conversion (cases/bottles, etc.) with base-unit weighted-average cost; dashboard today revenue / margin / purchase spend with drill-down
- Dual-track AI advisor (on-device Gemini Nano → cloud / proxy) plus RevenueCat subscription gating; core inventory free (up to 30 SKUs)
